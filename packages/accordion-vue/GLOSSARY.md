# Accordion

A reference for looking up which buttons and keys of a piano accordion make up
a scale, a chord or the chords of a key. The music theory words (scale, chord,
key, root and so on) are those of the
[music theory glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## The instrument

**Piano accordion**:
An accordion whose **Treble** is a keyboard of piano keys and whose left hand
plays a **Stradella bass**. The only kind of accordion the app shows.
_Avoid_: Accordion (on its own, when the kind matters), trekkspill

**Treble**:
The right-hand side of the accordion, which sounds single notes; on a **Piano
accordion**, 41 piano keys from F below middle C. Headed "Right hand" in the
app's text.
_Avoid_: Diskant, melody side

**Stradella bass**:
The left-hand side of a **Piano accordion**: 120 buttons in six **Rows** and
twenty **Columns**, the columns a fifth apart along the circle of fifths.
Headed "Left hand" in the app's text.
_Avoid_: Standard bass, bass side

**Row**:
The buttons of the **Stradella bass** that are all of one kind, running along
the circle of fifths: counterbass, fundamental bass, major, minor, seventh or
diminished.
_Avoid_: Line, rekke

**Column**:
The six buttons of the **Stradella bass** that belong to one **Fundamental
bass**: everything called C.
_Avoid_: Row (a row runs the other way)

## Buttons

**Bass button**:
A button of the **Stradella bass** that sounds a single note: a **Fundamental
bass** or a **Counterbass**.
_Avoid_: Bass key

**Chord button**:
A button of the **Stradella bass** that sounds a whole chord: major, minor,
seventh or diminished. The seventh and diminished buttons leave a note out: G7
sounds G B F, without its 5th.
_Avoid_: Chord key

**Fundamental bass**:
The **Bass button** with the note that the **Chord buttons** of its **Column**
are built on.
_Avoid_: Root bass, main bass

**Counterbass**:
The **Bass button** a major 3rd above a **Fundamental bass**, in the same
**Column** on the side nearest the bellows.
_Avoid_: Third bass

## Playing

**Combined chord**:
A chord with no **Chord button** of its own, played as a **Bass button** plus
the **Chord button** of another root: Cmaj7 is the C bass with the Em button.
_Avoid_: Compound chord, bass + chord

**Alternating bass**:
The left-hand pattern of a **Fundamental bass**, a **Chord button**, the bass a
fifth higher and the chord button again, one per beat.
_Avoid_: Oom-pah, vekselbass
