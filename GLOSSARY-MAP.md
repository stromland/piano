# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Music theory](./GLOSSARY.md): notes, scales, chords, keys and notation,
  shared by both apps
- [Piano](./packages/piano-vue/GLOSSARY.md): the piano keyboard
- [Accordion](./packages/accordion-vue/GLOSSARY.md): the piano accordion, its
  treble and its Stradella bass

## Relationships

- **Piano → Music theory** and **Accordion → Music theory**: both apps use the
  music theory words (scale, chord, key, root, inversion, grand staff) as they
  are defined there, and don't redefine them.
- **Accordion → Piano**: the **Treble** of a piano accordion is a keyboard of
  **Piano keys**.
