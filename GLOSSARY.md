# Piano

A reference for looking up which piano keys make up a scale or a chord, and
for learning how chords work together in a key so you can compose with them.

## Notes and the keyboard

**Piano key**:
One physical key on the keyboard, white or black.
_Avoid_: Key (on its own), tangent

**Pitch class**:
A note regardless of octave: every C on the keyboard is the same pitch class.

**Note name**:
How a pitch class is spelled in a given context, with a letter and any
accidentals: the same piano key is F♯ in D major and G♭ in D♭ major.
_Avoid_: Note label

**Interval**:
The distance from one note to another, named by degree and size: minor 3rd,
perfect 5th, major 7th.
_Avoid_: Gap

**Step**:
The distance between two neighboring notes of a scale: a half step (H) is one
piano key, a whole step (W) two.
_Avoid_: Tone, semitone (in the app's text)

**Root**:
The note a scale or chord is built up from and named after.
_Avoid_: Base note, root note (when it is a chord's)

## Scales

**Scale**:
A set of notes defined by its steps from the root, such as major, Dorian or
minor pentatonic. A scale is only notes; it says nothing about which chords
belong together.
_Avoid_: Key (when you mean a set of notes)

**Mode**:
One of the seven-note scales you get by starting the major scale on another
degree: Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian and Locrian. Ionian and Aeolian
are called major and minor.
_Avoid_: Church mode, view (a mode is never a screen of the app)

**Degree**:
A note's position in a scale counted from the root, written with an
accidental where it differs from major: 1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7.
_Avoid_: Step (a step is the distance between two notes)

## Chords

**Chord**:
Three or more notes sounded together, named by its root and chord type, such
as B♭m7.

**Chord type**:
The interval pattern that makes a chord what it is, regardless of root: major,
minor, diminished, augmented, sus2, sus4, maj7, 7, m7, m7♭5 or dim7.
_Avoid_: Quality (as a separate term), chord kind

**Triad**:
A chord of three notes.

**Seventh chord**:
A chord of four notes: a triad with a seventh on top.
_Avoid_: Four-note chord, tetrad

**Inversion**:
Which chord note is lowest. A chord has one inversion fewer than it has notes,
and an inverted chord keeps the name it was chosen by.
_Avoid_: Voicing (a voicing also spreads notes across octaves)

## Keys and harmony

**Key**:
A tonic plus a tonality, such as D minor, together with the chords a composer
uses in it. Different from a scale: the key of A minor includes E, borrowed from
harmonic minor, even though the natural minor scale gives Em.
_Avoid_: Scale (when you mean the chords), key signature

**Tonic**:
The root of a key, the note and chord that feel like home.

**Tonality**:
Whether a key is major or minor.
_Avoid_: Key signature, mode

**Roman numeral**:
A chord's degree in a key, upper case for major and lower case for minor, with
° for diminished, ø for half-diminished and + for augmented: I, ii, vii°, V7.

**Function**:
The role a chord plays in a key: tonic (rest), subdominant (moving away) or
dominant (tension that pulls back to the tonic). The VII of a minor key, a
whole step below the tonic, is called the subtonic.

**Borrowed chord**:
A chord that is used in a key but not built from its own scale; in this app,
the V and vii° that minor keys take from harmonic minor.
_Avoid_: Altered chord, chromatic chord
